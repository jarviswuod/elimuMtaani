elimuMtaani

Teach tomorrow's lesson tonight.

An AI-powered teaching assistant for Kenya's Competency-Based Curriculum — built for teachers and students.

The problem

Lesson prep eats evenings.
Help ends at the bell.

  • Teachers spend hours every week planning lessons against scarce, scattered curriculum resources.
  • Building a term timetable, slides, and a quiz from scratch is repeated, manual work every single week.
  • Once class ends, students have nowhere to ask "wait, why does that work?" until the next lesson.

The solution

One platform, research to delivered lesson.

Paste / upload research → AI term timetable → Narrated slides → Quiz → Classroom game

Plus Mwalimu Brain — a floating AI tutor available to both teachers and students, on every page, all the time.

Feature · Knowledge base

Real documents, real answers.

↑

Upload anything

PDF, DOCX, PPTX, CSV, or XLSX — teachers upload the materials they already have.

⚙

Parsed & embedded

Text is chunked and embedded with NVIDIA NIM embeddings into a searchable vector index.

🔎

Grounded retrieval

The chatbot and student practice quizzes pull the most relevant chunks — answers rooted in real source material.

Feature · Mwalimu Brain

An AI tutor that never sleeps.

  • A floating brain icon in the bottom-left corner — on every teacher and student page.
  • Powered by Claude Sonnet 5 — ask about any topic, any subject, anytime.
  • Microphone input with live transcription — just speak your question.
  • Spoken answers via NVIDIA voice synthesis, with an instant browser-speech fallback.

Feature · Games & leaderboards

Learning that competes for attention.

🎲

Classroom games

Library-first, AI-generated when needed — with a pre-class teacher review checklist before anything runs live.

🏆

Student leaderboard

Points from real quiz performance — every student sees their rank against classmates.

👥

Manage students

Teachers get a full roster view: who's active, who's improving, who needs a nudge.
